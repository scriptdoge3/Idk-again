OPERATING NOTES

Automatic start and stop

Put UNIT CONTROL and GOVERNOR in AUTO and the auxiliary switches on panel 1 in AUTO. Press START on panel 2.

The PLC starts cooling water and the HP lift pump, equalizes and opens the inlet valve, releases the brakes and opens the gates to 30 %. At 95 % speed it excites, then the auto-synchronizer closes 52G. With LEVEL CONTROL on, the PLC sets the load to hold the pond.

STOP unloads, opens 52G and closes the gates. The lift pump comes on below 90 % speed and the brakes apply at 25 %.

Manual synchronizing

Set SYNC MODE to MAN. At speed-no-load, match the incoming and running voltmeters with AVR SETPOINT and get the synchroscope turning slowly clockwise with SPEED / LOAD. Turn the 52G pistol grip to CLOSE just before 12 o'clock.

The 25 relay blocks the close outside ±15°, 0.2 Hz slip or 5 % voltage difference. SYNC CHECK BYPASS removes that protection.

Running a slow Francis
  • Below about 110 kW the unit is in its rough zone. Between 40 and 85 % of best-efficiency flow a vortex rope forms in the draft tube: watch the draft tube gauge, vibration and the kW needle swing. Draft tube air admission calms it.
  • Best efficiency is near 70 % gate (about 92 %). Full gate gives about 270 kW.
  • The thrust bearing has no oil film below roughly 12 % speed. Never turn the unit slowly without HP lift.
  • A full-load rejection overspeeds the unit to about 135 % and raises spiral case pressure about 4 psi.
After a trip

Read the annunciator and the protection relay targets. ACK the annunciator, fix the cause, reset 86G or 86M by clicking the lockout handle, press TARGET RESET on the relay, then START.

Moving around
  • Drag to look. W A S D or scroll to walk, Q E to turn, Shift to hurry.
  • Double-click a panel to step up to it. 1 2 3 go to the cabinets, 0 steps back.
  • Click the left or right side of a switch to turn it that way. Hold raise/lower switches.
  • On a phone: drag to look, pinch to walk, tap a device to see what it is.
